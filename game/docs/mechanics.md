# Flag Football Manager — Mechanics Reference

## Personalidade (Card Categories)

Three personality types define card categories, stat affinities, and vital costs:

| Personalidade | Abbrev | Color | Stats | Primary Vital Cost |
|---------------|--------|-------|-------|-------------------|
| **Intenso** | INT | Red | SPD, STR, STA | HP |
| **Tecnico** | TEC | Blue | AGI, DEX, BAL | NRG |
| **Estrategico** | EST | Green | PER, INT, CHA | SOC, LSR |

### Yomi (Rock-Paper-Scissors)

```
Intenso amassa Estrategico (+2 bonus / -1 penalty)
Estrategico le Tecnico (+2 bonus / -1 penalty)
Tecnico ginga de Intenso (+2 bonus / -1 penalty)
```

When playing a card against a situation:
- Card personality BEATS situation personality: +2 yomi bonus
- Card personality LOSES to situation personality: -1 yomi penalty
- Same personality (neutral): 0

## Vital Economy

| Vital | Abbrev | Used By | Regenerated By |
|-------|--------|---------|----------------|
| HP | HP | Intenso cards | Eating (HGR -> HP) |
| Energy | NRG | Tecnico cards | Eating (HGR -> NRG), Rest |
| Hunger | HGR | (indirect) | Fridge, delivery, snacks |
| Social | SOC | Estrategico cards | Social activities only |
| Leisure | LSR | Estrategico cards | Leisure activities only |
| Room | ROM | Casa cards | Cleaning room |

### Hunger as Indirect Resource

Food does not cost cards directly, but regenerates HP and NRG which are spent by cards. Without eating, HP/NRG drop, causing dice penalties. **Eating is deck maintenance.**

### Room (ROM) Cards

ROM enables "casa" (home) cards — practical resources found around your room:
- ROM high (organized room) = more casa cards available
- ROM low (messy room) = can't find anything useful

## Coin System

**1 coin = 5 vital points = +1 stat bonus**

Cards display coins in the top-right corner (Magic: The Gathering style). A 2-coin card costs 10 vital and gives +2 total stat bonus.

---

## d5* (Dice Five Star)

Six-sided die with faces 0-5. Explodes on 0 and 5:
- Roll 5: roll another die, ADD to result
- Roll 0: roll another die, SUBTRACT from result
- Explosions are recursive (5 -> 5 -> 3 = 13)

**Base roll: 2d5*** (two dice for symmetric distribution)

### Vital Penalty on Dice

The vital being spent determines how many dice you roll:

| Vital Level (of the type paid) | Dice |
|-------------------------------|------|
| 51-100 (healthy) | 2d5* normal |
| 26-50 (tired) | 1d5* only |
| 1-25 (exhausted) | 2d5* take the WORST |

Example: HP is 80, play card costing -5 HP -> roll 2d5* normally.
Example: HP is 15, play card costing -5 HP -> roll 2d5*, keep the lower result.

### Check Resolution

```
result = best_relevant_stat + card_bonuses + synergy + yomi + 2d5*(adjusted)
PASS if result >= difficulty
```

Where:
- `best_relevant_stat` = max(player_stat for each stat in check_stats)
- `card_bonuses` = sum of all played cards' stat_bonus (only for stats in check_stats)
- `synergy` = +1 per card with a matching situation_tag
- `yomi` = +2 or -1 per card based on personality vs situation personality
- `2d5*` = adjusted by the vital level of the cost paid

## Technique Cards

### Structure

```json
{
  "id": "burst_velocidade",
  "name": {"pt": "Burst de Velocidade", "en": "Speed Burst"},
  "desc": {"pt": "...", "en": "..."},
  "personality": "intenso",
  "tier": 1,
  "coins": 1,
  "vital_cost": {"hp": -5},
  "stat_bonus": {"speed": 1},
  "situation_tags": ["sprint_start", "burst"],
  "valid_contexts": ["drill", "training", "match"],
  "rarity": "common"
}
```

### Fields

- `personality`: intenso / tecnico / estrategico — determines yomi interactions
- `coins`: visual cost indicator (1 coin = 5 vital = +1 bonus)
- `vital_cost`: dictionary of vital costs (can span multiple vitals)
- `stat_bonus`: dictionary of stat bonuses gained when played
- `situation_tags`: tags that match with drill situations for +1 synergy each
- `valid_contexts`: where the card can appear in hand (drill, training, match, daily)
- `rarity`: common, uncommon, rare — affects acquisition

### Card Types

**Basic (1 coin)**: One stat +1. One per attribute (9 total).
**Advanced (2 coins)**: Two stats +1/+1. Cross-attribute combos (6 total).
**Casa (ROM)**: Practical home effects. Cost ROM vital.
**Meta**: Manipulate hand/table (draw cards, discard enemy cards, recover from graveyard).
**Match-only**: Only appear in match context.

## Hand Management

### Hand Size
- Maximum 5 cards visible
- Formula: `clampi(intelligence / 20, 1, 5)`
- Hand starts with 1 card drawn

### Draw Rate (cards are RARE)

| Intelligence | Draw Interval | Level |
|-------------|---------------|-------|
| 5-15 | 1 card / 5 ticks | Beginner |
| 16-35 | 1 card / 4 ticks | Average |
| 36-55 | 1 card / 3 ticks | Good |
| 56-80 | 1 card / 2 ticks | National |
| 81-100 | 2 cards / tick | Olympic |

Players must conserve cards for the right moment. Using everything leaves you vulnerable for several ticks.

### Multi-Card Play
- Play 0 to N cards per situation (all cards in hand if desired)
- Each card pays its vital cost separately
- Stat bonuses stack
- Yomi evaluated per card
- Played cards go to graveyard (not reshuffled during this drill)
- SKIP = play 0 cards, rely on raw stats + 2d5*

### Context Filtering
- Cards filter by current context (daily, drill, training, match)
- Cards outside context do NOT appear in hand
- Hand always shows max 5 relevant cards

### Graveyard
- Played cards go to graveyard for the duration of the drill/match
- Cognition meta cards can recover cards from graveyard

## Deck Building = Weekly Activity Planning

- Player HAS or DOES NOT HAVE a technique (binary)
- Training related activities add +1 copy of that card to the deck
- Example: trained "Hill Sprints" 3 times this week -> "Speed Burst" has 4 copies in deck (1 base + 3 training)
- More copies = higher chance of drawing that card
- **Deck building IS activity management**: plan your week to reinforce cards for the next drill/match

## Starter Deck

### "Who are you at school?"
Player chooses Origin (quebrada/condominio/mansao) x School (publico/bairro/elite) x Personality (intenso/tecnico/estrategico) = 27 possible combinations.

### Starting Cards (2 total)
1. **Base personality card**: generic 1-coin card from chosen personality
   - Intenso -> Speed Burst (+1 SPD)
   - Tecnico -> Light Step (+1 AGI)
   - Estrategico -> Pre-Read (+1 PER)

2. **Origin bonus**: unique card OR special bonus from origin x school combination

## Drills

Each drill is a sequence of 4 situations. Each situation is 1 tick.

### Situation Structure

```json
{
  "prompt": {"pt": "Saida! O apito toca.", "en": "Go! The whistle blows."},
  "check_stats": ["strength", "speed"],
  "personality": "intenso",
  "difficulty": 10,
  "situation_tags": ["sprint_start", "burst"],
  "viewport_action": {"type": "sprint", "from_yard": 0, "to_yard": 10}
}
```

### Physical Drills
- **40-Yard Dash**: 4 situations (start, stumble, acceleration, final sprint)
- **Three-Cone Drill**: 4 situations (sprint, 90-degree cut, round cone, sprint back)
- **Pro Agility (5-10-5)**: 4 situations (lateral burst, brake/reverse, sprint, final brake)

### Hotkeys
- **1-5**: play cards from hand
- **S**: skip (play no cards)
- **Q**: resolve next slot
- **W**: resolve full day
- **E**: resolve full week
- **F**: fridge window
- **M**: mirror window
- **SPACE**: pause
